Leek Soup

Description

This is a very simple soup recipe that I like to make in the fall.

Ingredients

  • 6–8 strips of bacon
  • 2–3 leeks
  • 3–4 potatoes - more if using smaller gold potatoes, fewer if using russet
  • 1 sweet onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • salt & pepper
  • chives - optional

Steps

  1. Fry bacon in pot.
  2. While bacon is frying, dice onion, chop leek, mince garlic, cube potatoes.
  3. When bacon is done, remove, then caramelize onion & leeks in bacon fat.
  4. Chop up the removed bacon.
  5. Add the garlic a little before the onion & leeks are done, to prevent burning.
  6. Add chicken stock, heavy cream, potatoes, bacon, salt & pepper.
  7. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer until the potatoes are soft.
  8. If you don't like potatoe chunks in your soup you can use an immersion blender.
  9. Serve, garnished with chives.
