Chili

Description

This is one of my most made recipes, especially in the fall and winter. It tastes significantly better than canned chili and is pretty easy to adjust to your favorite flavors as you continue making new batches.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 pound 90% lean ground beef - I've used other fat ratios without a problem, but if you're using beef with more fat, I'd recommend browning it separately and draining it before adding it to the other ingredients.
  • 1 medium yellow onion - diced
  • 2 1/2 tbsp chili powder - different brands have very large differences in spice level
  • 2 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper - optional, but I always include it
  • 1 1/2 cups beef broth
  • 1 can (15 oz.) petite diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (16 oz.) red kidney beans - drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (8 oz.) tomato sauce

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large soup pot on medium-high heat. Add the onion. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add the ground beef. Cook until browned, stirring occasionally; about 6–7 mins.
  3. Add the chili powder, cumin, sugar, tomato paste, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and cayenne. Stir until well combined.
  4. Add the broth, diced tomatoes, drained beans, and tomato sauce. Stir well.
  5. Bring to a low boil. Reduce heat to gently simmer, uncovered, for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Remove from heat. Let rest for 5–10 minutes before serving.
